## Starting Point

It wasn't agentic AI from the beginning. It started with computer vision — detecting people in indoor spaces, tracking movement, estimating crowd density.

The work was concrete: YOLOv8m for detection, OC-SORT for tracking, Edge Density for estimation. When it all came together as a working system, something clicked.

## The Question

After building the crowd density pipeline, one question kept coming back:

> "The system sees. But what does it *do* next?"

Every response was hardcoded — thresholds, rules, static logic. The system couldn't adapt. It could observe but not reason.

## The Turn

At KINGPC, I sat through a talk on Agentic AI. The framing was simple: CV perceives, LLM reasons, agent acts.

That was the missing piece. Not a better model — a better *system*.
